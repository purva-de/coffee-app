Frequently Asked Questions

When were my coffee beans roasted?

Every single bag of Coffee Co. is stamped with its exact roast date. We never warehouse roasted beans; your order is roasted to order and leaves our facility within 48 hours.

What grind should I choose for an AeroPress or Pour Over?

Select our "Medium (Drip / Chemex / V60)" option for AeroPress, Chemex, and V60. If you prefer high extraction and intense body, choose "Fine Espresso".

Do you offer free shipping on coffee orders?

Yes! All orders over ₹799 qualify for 100% free priority roastery shipping directly to your doorstep across India.
